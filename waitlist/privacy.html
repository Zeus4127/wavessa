Wavessa privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version: Wavessa keeps everything on your phone. We don't collect, see, sell or share any of your information.

What the app stores

Anything you type or add in the app stays in the app's storage on your device. That includes your routines, session log, check-ins, sleep diary, medicines list, habits, goals, notes and photos.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The Android app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your information to us or anyone else.

Links that leave the app

Some buttons open other apps or websites, such as adding a reminder to Google Calendar or calling a phone number. If you use them, that app or website's own privacy policy applies.

Backups

You can save a backup file or copy a backup code. You choose where the file goes, such as your phone's storage or a cloud drive. Keep it somewhere safe, because anyone with the file can read what's in it.

Deleting your information

You can delete a person and their records inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.

Children

Wavessa is made for adults aged 18 and over.

Health information

Wavessa is a wellness and routine app. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and isn't a substitute for advice from a doctor. In an emergency in Australia, call 000.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this website, your email and any answers you choose to give are sent to us by email, using the free form service FormSubmit. We use them only to tell you about Wavessa and to decide what to build first. We never sell or share them. Ask us at any time and we will delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Email wavessaa@outlook.com.
