Questions

Good to know.

What is a seat session?

A flat copper coil inside the cushion makes a gentle pulsed electromagnetic field (often called PEMF) in a pattern that matches the mood you pick. Most people describe it as a light, steady rhythm. It is a relaxation and wellness experience, not a medical treatment.

Is it safe for everyone?

Do not use the seat pad or our devices if you have a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device, or if you are pregnant, unless your doctor says it is fine. Sessions are for adults only. If you have a health condition, talk to your doctor first. You can stop any session yourself, and it stops as soon as you stand up.

Does it treat any health problem?

No. Our seats and devices are general wellness products. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and our staff will not give medical advice.

Do I have to have a session?

No. It is a normal cafe too. The pad only runs when you choose a session from the menu.

Can I buy a device today?

Not yet. Devices are in development with a Bangkok manufacturer. Reserving is free, and we will contact you with the final price and a collection date before you pay anything. Prices shown are targets and include GST.

Where is it?

Bangkok. We are choosing the street now and will tell everyone on the invite list first, along with the pop-up dates.
