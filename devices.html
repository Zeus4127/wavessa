Questions

Good to know.

What does a frequency device do?

It makes a gentle pulsed electromagnetic field (often called PEMF) from a flat copper coil, in patterns named after moods like Calm and Focus. People use it as part of their relaxation and wellbeing routine. It is a wellness product, not a medical treatment.

Is it safe for everyone?

Do not use our devices if you have a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device, or if you are pregnant, unless your doctor says it is fine. They are for adults. If you have a health condition, talk to your doctor first.

Does it treat any health problem?

No. Our devices are general wellness products. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

Can I buy one today?

Not yet. Devices are in development with a Bangkok manufacturer. Reserving is free, and we will contact you with the final price and a collection date before you pay anything. Prices are targets and include 7% VAT.

Can I try one first?

Yes. Every device will be on the counter at the Studio, and owners get free seat sessions there.
