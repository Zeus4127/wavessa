A calm you can carry. Even into the surf.

The Mini Pod is a pocket-sized frequency device with a round touchscreen, Bluetooth, back-to-back programs and an IP68 waterproof target. Smaller, tougher and better value than the one you've seen.

Most frequency devices ask you to sit still and remember what comes next. This one doesn't.

The Mini Pod reads how settled you are, queues your routine in order and keeps going in the rain, at the beach and on the pillow.

It runs the same five moods as the seats in the Wavessa Studio: Calm, Focus, Recharge, Unwind and Deep Rest.
