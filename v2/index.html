One room, three worlds. Pick the one that fits your day.

Wavessa Studio is a small Bangkok cafe built around one idea: a calm seat. Every chair has a pad you can feel, running one of five moods while you drink your coffee.

In the morning it's a bright cafe. At five the lights drop and it becomes a quiet lounge. And at the side there's a little shop where you can hold the Mini Pod and the rest of the range.
