Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing on its own. There are no accounts, no tracking cookies, no ads and no analytics.

Booking and the founding list

When you send a seat booking or join the founding list, your own email app opens with the details filled in. We only see what you choose to send. We use it to reply to you and to tell you when we open, and we never sell or share it.

Saved on your device

The site remembers whether you picked day or night for this visit, and it keeps a copy of itself on your device so it opens without signal. You can clear both in your browser settings.

Other services

Fonts load from Google Fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Asking or removing your details

Email hello@example.com and we'll tell you what we hold, or delete it.
