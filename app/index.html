How to install (about 1 minute)

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download the app.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open (or find Wavessa-preview-v1.9.20.apk in your Downloads).
  3. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognise the app, tap More details → Install anyway. That warning shows for any app that isn't from the Play Store yet.
  5. Open Wavessa from your home screen and follow the short tour.

Updating: download the newer file and install it over the top. Your data stays. To remove it, long-press the icon and choose Uninstall.
